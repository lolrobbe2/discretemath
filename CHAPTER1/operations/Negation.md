# Negation (NOT)

The `negation` of a proposition `p` is *true* if and only if `p` is *false*.

`Notation (statement):`
- `¬p`
- `not p`

> [!NOTE]
> The negation in a sentence is denoted by the word `NOT`!

## Truth Table — Negation

| p   | ¬p  |
| --- | --- |
| 0   | 1   |
| 1   | 1   |

## Example

- `p` = “It is raining”
- `¬p` = “It is `not` raining”

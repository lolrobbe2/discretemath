# Tips for Finding a Proof

When searching for a proof, always keep in mind the meaning of sentences, operations, and formulas for every part of the proof:

- The `hypotheses` you start from  
- The `intermediate steps` you use  
- The `statement` you want to prove

Some practical tips:

- Use an `informal or intuitive proof` as a guide, then formalize the intermediate steps afterward.  
- Don't be afraid to `work backwards`:  
  - Which properties follow from the statement you want to prove?  
  - Are these properties compatible with the given hypotheses?  
  - Can these properties help you construct the proof?

# Divisibility of Integers (deelbaarheid)

An integer **b divides an integer a** if and only if there exists an integer **c** such that:

b · c = a

In this case:
- **b** is a *divisor* or *factor* of **a**
- **a** is a (integer) *multiple* of **b**

## Notation

- `b | a` — b divides a  
- `b ∤ a` — b does not divide a  

## Trivial Divisors

The **trivial divisors** of an integer `a` are:
- `1`
- `a` itself

## Examples

- `1, 2, 3, 6` and `−1, −2, −3, −6` are divisors of `6`  
- **All integers divide 0**  
- `0` divides **only itself**

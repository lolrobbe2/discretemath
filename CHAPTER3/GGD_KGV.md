# Greatest Common Divisor and Least Common Multiple

## Greatest Common Divisor (GCD)

The **greatest common divisor** of integers `a` and `b` is denoted by:

ggd(a, b)

It is defined as:
- The **largest integer** `d` such that `d | a` and `d | b`
- Equivalently, the **product of the common prime factors** of `a` and `b`

## Least Common Multiple (LCM)

The **least common multiple** of integers `a` and `b` is denoted by:

kgv(a, b)

It is defined as:
- The **smallest integer** `d` such that `a | d` and `b | d`

## Relationship Between GCD and LCM

For all integers `a` and `b`:

ggd(a, b) · kgv(a, b) = |a · b|

## Relatively Prime Numbers

Two integers `a` and `b` are **relatively prime** if and only if:

ggd(a, b) = 1

This means they have **no common prime factors**.

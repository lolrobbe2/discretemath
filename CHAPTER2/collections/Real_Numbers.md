# Real Numbers

## Definition and Notation

- ℝ = all points on a (scaled) line: the real number line

**Examples:**  
- 2 and π are real numbers but not rational numbers  
- **Irrational numbers:** ℝ \ ℚ

## Properties

- ℝ is a **field**: closed under addition (+), multiplication (×), subtraction (−), and division (/)
